# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the Plivo CLI project — issues, discussions, and reviews — to be a harassment-free, welcoming, and professional experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior we expect:

- Be respectful, considerate, and collaborative.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Show empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment of any kind, whether public or private.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information (such as a physical or electronic address) without explicit permission.
- Any other conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)** as the detailed standard. Please read it for the full text.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any communication channel used for project coordination. It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces (e.g., using a `@plivo.com` email address tied to the project, posting via an official social account, or speaking at an event as a project representative).

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported confidentially to the project maintainers at **opensource@plivo.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the maintainers will respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may take appropriate corrective action — including editing or removing contributions, or restricting participation — in response to behavior they deem inappropriate.
