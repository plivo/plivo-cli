# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the Plivo CLI project — issues, discussions, and reviews
— to be a harassment-free, welcoming, and professional experience for everyone,
regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

We expect everyone interacting in this project's spaces to:

- Be respectful, considerate, and collaborative.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

Behavior that a reasonable person would consider harassment, discrimination, or
otherwise inappropriate in a professional setting is not tolerated.

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as the detailed standard for expected and unacceptable behavior. Please read it
for the full text.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported confidentially to the project maintainers at **opensource@plivo.com**.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the
maintainers will respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may
take appropriate corrective action — including editing or removing contributions,
or restricting participation — in response to behavior they deem inappropriate.
